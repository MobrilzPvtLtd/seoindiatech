## Interpretation

Google is already **testing the site** for relevant commercial queries (SEO Company India, SEO Services in India, Technical SEO, Local SEO, E-commerce SEO, winery-related terms, etc.). The gap is **not discovery** — it is **rank depth**, **CTR**, and **intent alignment** on URLs that already earn impressions.

## Measurable Goals (not guaranteed rankings)

Phase 13 targets **measurable movement**, not guaranteed Page 1:

1. Increase **clicks** from existing impression base
2. Increase **CTR** on high-impression URLs with weak CTR
3. Improve **average position** on priority industry URLs (67–80 → toward 30 → 20)
4. Increase **Page 1 keyword count** as positions improve
5. Increase **commercial-query visibility** on mapped money pages
6. Reduce **irrelevant URL impressions** via internal linking (not noindex)
7. Increase **industry-page visibility** for data-driven Top 5
