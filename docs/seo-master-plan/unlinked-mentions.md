## Reclamation Playbook (Human-Approved Only)

### When mention is positive and contextual:

1. Thank the author publicly (LinkedIn comment) if appropriate
2. Send brief email:

```
Subject: Thank you for mentioning SEO India Tech

Hi [Name],

Thank you for referencing SEO India Tech in [article title]. We appreciate the mention.

If helpful for your readers, a link to [specific relevant page] might provide additional context on [topic]:
[URL]

No pressure either way — thanks again for including us.

Best,
[Name]
```

### When mention is inaccurate:

- Correct factually — do not argue for links
- Escalate misattribution (e.g., confused with seoindia.co) politely

### When NOT to outreach:

- Negative reviews (respond professionally, no link ask)
- Spam forums
- Paid directory upsells
- Mentions that shouldn't exist (fake listings) — request removal instead
