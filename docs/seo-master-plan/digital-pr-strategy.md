## Guest Contribution Policy

### Pursue only when:

- Website is relevant to SEO, marketing, business, or technology
- Publication is legitimate with editorial standards
- Audience matches SEO India Tech ICP (SMB, agency buyers, marketing managers)
- Content is genuinely useful — not advertorial
- Link is editorially appropriate (brand, URL, or descriptive partial match)

### Reject:

- PBNs, link farms, automated guest-post networks
- Sites created solely for SEO links
- Irrelevant "write for us" pages with excessive outbound links
- Exact-match commercial anchor requirements

### Preferred anchors in guest content:

- "SEO India Tech"
- "this guide from SEO India Tech"
- `https://www.seoindiatech.com/blog/...`
- Descriptive: "technical SEO checklist"

## Outreach Quality Standards

### Each pitch must include:

1. **Why this publication** — specific section/audience fit
2. **What value for readers** — education, data, or tool
3. **What asset** — URL to specific guide/tool
4. **Why SEO India Tech** — relevant expertise (factual only)
5. **No link entitlement** — link is editorial decision

### Never use:

- "Dear webmaster"
- "Please add our link"
- "Can you give me a backlink?"
- Mass mail-merge without customization
- Paid link offers without `rel="sponsored"`

### Sample structure (customize per prospect):

```
Subject: Resource for [Publication] readers — [specific topic]

Hi [Name],

I read your [specific article/resource page] on [topic]. Your section on [X] aligns with work we've published on [specific challenge].

We recently published a [guide/checklist] covering [specific value]:
[URL]

It includes [concrete benefit — e.g., 40-point technical audit checklist] that [publication audience] might find useful when [use case].

If it's a fit for your resources, we'd appreciate consideration. Happy to adapt format or provide an expert quote on [timely topic].

[Name]
[Title], SEO India Tech
[Verified credentials only]
```
